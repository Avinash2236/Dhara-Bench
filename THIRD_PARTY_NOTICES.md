# Third-party corpus materials

The project licences in [LICENSE-CODE](LICENSE-CODE) and
[LICENSE-DATA](LICENSE-DATA) cover the original contributions identified in
[LICENSE](LICENSE). They do not relicense the source-derived text in
`chunks.json`, third-party quotations in questions, or the underlying editions,
translations, and commentaries.

[source_metadata.csv](source_metadata.csv) supplies bibliographic descriptions
and acquisition URLs. It has no fields establishing source licences,
public-domain determinations, or redistribution permissions. The entries below
record that gap; they do not assert that a particular source is restricted or
that permission has been obtained. An accessible URL or a bibliographic date
alone is not a verified redistribution basis. OCR, segmentation, and machine
translation do not by themselves establish rights in the source material.

For each edition or resource, record the applicable licence, public-domain
basis, or permission and its scope before describing its redistribution rights
as verified. Retain any required attribution and licence notices. If a source
group contains several editions, their rights must be recorded separately.
